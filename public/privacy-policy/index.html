Privacy Policy

GatedCart is a community marketplace operated by Cheritech. This policy explains how we collect, use, store and protect information when you use the GatedCart application and website.

Information we collect

We may collect your name, mobile number, email address, apartment and delivery details, saved addresses, orders, seller applications, support messages, device and notification identifiers, and information you provide when using marketplace services.

How we use information

We use this information to authenticate your account, show sellers and community services available to your apartment, process orders, provide delivery updates, send notifications you enable, prevent misuse, provide support, and meet legal and accounting obligations.

Sharing and service providers

We share information only as needed to operate the service, such as with the seller or delivery provider involved in your order, hosting and authentication providers, notification services, and authorities where legally required. We do not sell your personal information.

Security and retention

We use reasonable technical and organizational safeguards. We retain information only for as long as needed for the purposes described here, legal requirements, dispute resolution, fraud prevention, and legitimate business records.

Your choices and account deletion

You can submit an account deletion request from the dedicated account deletion page. The request is recorded for review. Associated personal data will be deleted when the request is completed, except information we must retain by law or for legitimate records.

Contact

For privacy questions or deletion requests, contact Cheritech at vmydealer@gmail.com.
